## Goal

Produce video assets that meet their delivery needs without losing source quality.

## When to use

- Encoding a recording for documentation or sharing.
- Trimming and assembling short clips from longer sources.
- Adding captions or transcripts to visual material.

## Steps

1. Define the delivery target, duration, resolution, and expected viewing context.
2. Keep source files immutable and write derivatives to a named output area.
3. Describe encoding choices generically and treat any tool as an example.
4. Check frame boundaries, audio sync, and transitions before a full batch.
5. Prefer compact output settings that preserve legibility of text and diagrams.
6. Provide captions or a transcript for spoken content.
7. Note PowerShell and POSIX quoting differences for documented commands.

## Verification

- The output plays correctly and matches the documented technical properties.
- A spot check confirms audio and visual elements remain synchronized.
- No personal metadata or sensitive information remains in shared files.
