---
name: Research Workflow
description: Gather authoritative evidence before making a design, configuration, or documentation decision.
---

## Goal

Base decisions on verifiable sources and keep a durable record of what was checked.

## When to use

- Before changing configuration, APIs, or tool behavior.
- When documentation must reflect current upstream guidance.
- When competing recommendations need to be compared fairly.

## Steps

1. Restate the question and list the specific facts that must be verified.
2. Prefer official documentation, then upstream source, then reputable references.
3. Record the exact address, access date, and the fact each source supports.
4. Require at least two authoritative sources for tooling or API decisions.
5. Separate verified facts from assumptions and unresolved questions.
6. Summarize the findings and the exact proposed change before acting on it.
7. Update the research record with the outcome after implementation.

## Verification

- Every claim in the proposal links to a recorded source.
- Source quality and access dates are visible to reviewers.
- The final record describes what was done and how it was verified.
